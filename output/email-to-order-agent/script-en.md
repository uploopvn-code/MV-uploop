# Email-to-Order Agent

60-second product explainer. All interfaces and order data are illustrative.

## 00-10s: From emails to WMS orders

Meet the Email-to-Order Agent. It turns shipping instructions from emails into warehouse orders, reducing repetitive data entry for your operations team.

## 10-20s: One agent. Multiple email sources.

The agent reads emails from customers, freight forwarders, and factory sites, including purchase orders, shipping instructions, and inbound delivery advice.

## 20-30s: Understand the request

It identifies inbound and outbound shipping intent, then extracts key order details, such as purchase order number, part number, quantity, and destination.

## 30-40s: Check details. Flag exceptions.

The agent validates order information and requests missing details. If order creation fails, a notification helps the team identify what needs attention.

## 40-50s: Create the WMS order

With the required information available, the agent creates the warehouse order in WMS and sends confirmation, helping the team prepare for warehouse operations.

## 50-60s: Less manual work. More connected operations.

Support covers Crane customers and warehouse transfers in Mexico, including a second warehouse. The benefits: faster order creation, fewer manual input errors, and cross-warehouse automation.
